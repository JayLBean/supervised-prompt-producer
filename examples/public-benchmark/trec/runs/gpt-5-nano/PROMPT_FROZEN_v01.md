<rules>
- Description: the answer is an explanation, method, reason, function, manner, or a definition/classification of a specific named or singular subject — what something means (for an ordinary word), why, how, the best way to do something, or what a particular language/band/product/named thing is.
- Entity: the answer is a concrete item nameable in a few words — a named term/word/name for something, a color, a substance/material/chemical, a food/plant/animal, a phrase or set of words, a phobia/fear-term, or what something is made of, contains, or eats. Use Entity (not Description) when the answer is such a thing, judging by answer shape and ignoring the "what is/was/were" verb.
- Composition/consumption questions — "what is X made of", "what does X consist of/contain", "what do X eat" — name a substance, material, or food and are Entity, not Description. (Keep distinct from definitional "what is <named subject>" which stays Description.)
- Expression: the answer is an abbreviation or its expansion ONLY — the subject is an all-caps initialism, or the question contains "stand for", "abbreviation of", "short for", or "what does <ACRONYM> mean". Not for "term/word/name/fear of X" (Entity) or "meaning of <ordinary word>" (Description).
- Human: the answer is a person, king, fictional character, a person's name or name-part (first/middle/last name, nickname), a profession or title, or any NAMED collective of people acting as a unit — company, corporation, band/named group, sports team, police/military/guerrilla force, organization, club, or newsgroup/community — including when introduced by "what group/company/force/team/band." A named company or group is Human, not Entity.
- Location: the answer names or derives from a place — country, city, state, river, sea, lake, ocean, mountain, waterway/canal/road, island, region, building, library, monument, landmark, geographic feature, the location where an event occurred, or a nationality/origin — even when phrased "what river/mountain/country/city/nationality/library …" or asking for the largest/longest/highest/closest such place. "Where …" and "what city/country/nationality …" default to Location even when the sentence names a concrete object; that named object is the place, not the answer.
- Number: the answer is a quantitative value — count, date/date-of-birth, year, distance, dimension, money, temperature/oven setting, unit of weight/measure, or the time/season for doing something — even without an explicit "how many/how much."
- Tie-breaker: when two classes seem plausible, apply by cue strength — an explicit place cue (Where / what city/country/nationality) → Location; an explicit person/group/profession/name-part cue → Human; otherwise judge Entity vs Description by answer shape. Classify by the type of answer expected, not the question's topic; topic words never override the answer-type cue, and when a place, person, or thing appears only in the setup, label by what the answer itself will be. Additionally: (a) a named company/organization/team/band/guerrilla-military group is HUMAN even when phrased "the X that did Y" or treated as a thing; (b) a country/city/place answer is LOCATION even when a specific named place or object is what's asked for.
