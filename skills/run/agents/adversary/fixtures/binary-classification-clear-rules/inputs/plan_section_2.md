# plan.md §2 — Class definitions (excerpt)

## Relevant

A ticket whose **primary subject** is the billing product —
invoices, charges, refunds, subscription state, payment
methods, pricing, billing-cycle mechanics, or any account
state that is gated by billing (e.g., access locked due to
failed payment).

The intuitive test: would a billing-team triager pick this up
from a shared queue?

## Not Relevant

A ticket whose primary subject is anything other than the
billing product — login mechanics not caused by billing,
feature requests, general account questions, product usage
questions unrelated to pricing or payment.
